Fluorescence excitation-emission data for GltJ, a candidate flavoprotein,
recorded by Chloe (Sturgis group) in October 2025 and used with permission.

J_Peri.csv   173 columns as (wavelength, intensity) pairs -- one emission
             spectrum per excitation wavelength, 290-460 nm in 5 nm steps,
             for oxidised and reduced samples.

The layout is what a spectrofluorimeter exports: a sparse first header row
naming each series, a second row labelling the columns, then paired data.
